Helios Robotics SA

Helios Robotics, founded in 2016 by Dr. Amara Okafor, is headquartered in Lyon, France. Okafor still serves as chief executive officer of the company.

The company's flagship product, the Atlas Arm, is a collaborative industrial robot arm used in automotive and electronics assembly lines.

Helios sources the battery packs of the Atlas Arm from Lumen Batteries S.p.A.
